# NFS MW 3DS: v0.18 exploration preview

Extract the ZIP to the SD card root, keeping every included file together.
Launch `3ds/nfsmw-rockport/nfsmw-rockport.3dsx` from Homebrew Launcher.
Keep your existing `aliases.sav`; no test Alias or career save is included.

Press Start and create or load an Alias. On the main menu, Y starts unlocked
Rockport exploration. Circle Pad steers, A accelerates, B brakes, Y reverses,
R applies the handbrake, and X resets to the starting road.

Inactive race arrow walls no longer render or collide in exploration. Source
assets remain available for race activation. This exploration shortcut has no
career district locks; it does not complete or unlock a saved career.

Near original race entries, parts shops, safehouses and car lots, D-pad Up opens
the destination screen. Start opens Pause, including Jump to Safe House. Resume
keeps the current car position. Jump to Safe House currently opens the safehouse
menu; it does not move the car to a different physical location.
Original minimap icons mark supported race entries, shops, car lots and safehouses
when they fall within the displayed area.

The top display shows original artwork, Alias context and the live BMW preview;
the bottom display handles navigation. The minimap road drawing remains partial.

This is an unfinished preview: race starts/opponents/rewards, shop purchases and
upgrades, complete car selection, career/story/cutscene playback and original
audio are not implemented. Handling and camera remain provisional. Four original
safehouse regions and the former starting barrier have been checked; every city
route and stock-hardware performance have not been validated.
